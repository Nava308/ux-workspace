## Logo Rules

- **Primary format**: Linear (horizontal) — default for all media
- **Stacked format**: Only when space is constrained
- **Safe space**: Minimum 2x clear space on all sides (where x = height of 'G' is 3x)
- **Minimum size (linear)**: 49.5mm / 4.95cm / 584.65px @300dpi
- **Minimum size (stacked)**: 24.8mm / 2.48cm / 292.91px @300dpi

### Logo Don'ts
- Do not change alignment
- Do not rotate or change orientation
- Do not place logo on images/photos
- Do not change colours outside palette
- Do not distort or change proportions
- Do not clip textures or images into logo
- Do not use on unapproved coloured backgrounds
- Do not use outlines or effects on logo
- Do not create custom variations or lockups

### Logo Lockups
1. **University lockup** — official comms, academic docs, website, partnerships, signage
2. **Cities lockup** — external events, education fairs, off-campus branding, PR, marketing
3. **Basic identity** — merchandise, internal comms, school branding, facilities

## Brand Imagery
- Positive, confident, cheerful individuals
- Not cluttered or intimidating
- Well-focused, sharp, bright, expressive
- Must align with the brand colour palette for visual cohesion
- Facility/equipment images must highlight a key element meaningfully

## Tone of Voice

### Principles
- Inspiring · Caring · Morally Strong · Humility

### Voice Qualities
1. **Optimistic** — confident, positive, active, to the point (never unrealistic)
2. **Exceptional** — positive, assured, focused, decisive (never boastful)
3. **Agile** — warm, helpful, factual (never unsteady)
4. **Goal-oriented** — informed, inspiring, confident, clear (never confusing)

### Writing Best Practices
- Clear and simple words
- Present factual data with numbers and proofs
- Short, crisp sentences
- Contractions where appropriate (it's, we're)
- Positive language, active voice

### Audience Tone Guide
| Audience             | Tone                    | Language             | Emphasis                        |
|----------------------|-------------------------|----------------------|---------------------------------|
| Undergraduate        | Positive & Enthusiastic | Short sentences      | Excited and energised           |
| Postgraduate         | Sophisticated           | Specialized language | Concise and jargon-free         |
| International        | Warm & Welcoming        | Confident language   | Simple and to the point         |
| Alumni               | Inclusive               | Sophisticated        | Concise and jargon-free         |
| Corporate / Partners | Formal                  | Sophisticated        | Innovation and world impact      |

### Things to Avoid
- Stuffy or old-fashioned language
- Boastful or institutional tone
- Overly formal or boring content
- Long-winded phrases (e.g. say "we can help you choose" not "the staff will be able to assist you in choosing")
